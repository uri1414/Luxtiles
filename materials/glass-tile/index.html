One practical note: because glass is translucent, the setting material shows through and clean cuts matter, so it installs a little more exactingly than standard ceramic. We'll advise on setting materials and grout, and it's worth using an installer comfortable with glass.

Sizes, sheets, and grout

Glass comes in everything from tiny mosaics mounted on mesh sheets to larger field tiles and linear planks, and the format changes the feel: small mosaics read as texture and shimmer, while larger pieces read as clean, continuous color. Because glass is translucent, two details matter more than with ceramic — the setting material shows through, so a white or light-bodied thinset keeps colors true, and grout color noticeably shifts the final look, so it's worth choosing grout against a sample rather than after the fact. We'll walk you through sizes and grout options so the tile you picture is the tile you get, and confirm sheet counts so a mosaic backsplash or waterline comes out even.

Glass tile FAQ

Where does glass tile work best?
Glass shines wherever you want light, color, and easy cleaning: kitchen backsplashes, pool and spa waterlines, showers, and feature walls. Because it's non-porous it resists stains and moisture, which is why it's a favorite behind cooktops and underwater. It's typically a decorative/vertical and waterline material rather than a heavy-traffic floor.
What are glass tile blends?
A blend mixes several glass colors, finishes, or sizes into one coordinated tile or mosaic sheet, so a backsplash or waterline has depth and movement instead of one flat color. Custom blends are our specialty — we can help you dial in a mix that ties your counters, cabinets, or pool finish together.
Is glass tile hard to install or maintain?
Maintenance is easy — it wipes clean and doesn't need sealing. Installation is a bit more exacting than standard ceramic because glass is translucent (the setting material shows through) and needs clean cuts, so it's worth using an installer comfortable with glass. We're glad to advise on setting materials and grout.

Yes — wholesale/builder pricing and job-site delivery across Greater Los Angeles, from a single backsplash sheet count to a full pool waterline.
