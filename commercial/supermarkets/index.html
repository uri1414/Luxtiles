How we supply a supermarket build

Grocery construction moves on a tight, phased schedule, and the tile can't be what holds it up. We start with a material takeoff you can build your bid on, lock in wholesale pricing, and hold the inventory so a store's worth of tile is guaranteed to match across every aisle — no dye-lot surprises halfway through. Then we stage delivery to the build: sales floor when the slab's ready, wet zones and back-of-house as those areas come online, entries last. With 1,000+ projects supplied and a deep in-stock inventory, large orders don't stall, and one point of contact stays with the job from bid through final delivery — reliable, insured, and built to keep your trades moving.

Supermarket tile FAQ

Is your tile durable enough for a supermarket floor?
Yes — supermarket floors are exactly what large-format porcelain and other commercial-rated tile are built for. We help you spec surfaces that take carts, foot traffic, and daily cleaning while staying easy to maintain across a big footprint.
Can you handle slip resistance for wet and food areas?
We match materials to each zone. Produce, deli, and entry areas call for textured, slip-appropriate finishes, while the main sales floor can use a smoother large-format surface. Tell us the layout and we'll spec accordingly.
Do you offer builder pricing and staged delivery for a full store?
Yes. Supermarkets are quoted at wholesale/builder rates, and we stage and schedule delivery so material is on site as each area is ready — with takeoffs to support your bid.
