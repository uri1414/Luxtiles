How we supply a shopping-center build

Retail centers are built in phases and leased in pieces, so the tile plan has to flex. We give you a takeoff for the bid, builder pricing on the volume, and enough held inventory that the common-area surface stays consistent across a concourse no matter how long the build runs. Storefront and tenant spaces get sourced to their own looks as they're finalized, and we stage delivery by area or phase so nothing sits on a busy site longer than it has to. One insured partner coordinates the shared surfaces while each space keeps its identity — with a single point of contact from bid to completion.

Shopping-center tile FAQ

Can your tile handle shopping-center foot traffic?
Yes — concourses and common areas are prime territory for large-format porcelain, which gives a clean, continuous look and stands up to constant foot traffic with easy maintenance across a big footprint.
Can you supply varied looks for different tenants and storefronts?
We can. Shopping centers mix a durable common-area surface with storefront and tenant spaces that each want their own look — and our range covers both. We'll help coordinate the shared areas while giving each space its own character.
Do you offer builder pricing and phased delivery?
Yes. Shopping-center work is quoted at builder/wholesale rates, and we stage delivery by area or phase so material lands as each space is ready — with takeoffs to support the bid.
