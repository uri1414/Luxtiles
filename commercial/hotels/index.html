How we supply a hotel build

Hospitality projects juggle brand standards, a design vision, and a hard opening date, so the tile package has to be predictable. We work from your standard or design intent to source materials that hit both the look and the performance spec, provide takeoffs for the bid, and price the volume at builder rates. Then we hold inventory and stage delivery to the schedule — often phased by floor or by area on a larger property — so guest floors, public spaces, and back-of-house each get their material as they're ready. One insured supplier keeps the finish coordinated from lobby to back-of-house, with a single point of contact through completion.

Hotel tile FAQ

Can you meet hotel brand standards?
Yes. Hospitality projects come with brand and design standards, and we help you source tile and stone that hits the look and the performance spec — luxury where guests see it, durability where it's used hard. Share the standard or the design intent and we'll match it.
Do you supply both guest-facing and back-of-house areas?
We supply the whole property — lobbies, feature walls, guest rooms and baths, corridors, spa and pool, and back-of-house. One supplier keeps the look coordinated and the pricing consistent.
Do you offer volume pricing and scheduled delivery for hotels?
Yes. Hotels are quoted at builder/wholesale rates, and we stage delivery to your construction schedule — phased by floor or area on larger builds — with takeoffs to support the bid.
