Commercial FAQ

Do you offer builder and wholesale pricing on commercial tile?
Yes. Commercial and multi-unit projects are priced at builder/wholesale rates, and pricing improves with volume. Send us the scope — square footage, surfaces, and timeline — and we'll put together a project quote.
Can you handle large-format tile and high-traffic specifications?
Large-format tile is a core part of what we supply, and it's a common commercial spec because fewer grout lines mean a cleaner look and easier maintenance. We help match materials to traffic and wear requirements so the floor or wall holds up in a supermarket aisle, a hotel lobby, or an apartment common area.
Do you deliver to commercial job sites?
Yes — job-site delivery is included across Greater Los Angeles, and we coordinate deliveries to your schedule so material is on site when your crews need it and phased across a larger build if required.
Can you support a project from bidding through completion?
That's how we prefer to work. We provide material takeoffs and pricing to support your bid, hold and stage inventory, and stay involved through completion so you have one reliable tile-and-stone partner for the whole job.
