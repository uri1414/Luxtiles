How we supply a multifamily build

Multifamily runs in phases and repeats the same units hundreds of times, so consistency and scheduling are everything. We provide a per-unit takeoff to support your bid, price the volume at builder rates, and reserve inventory so every bathroom and kitchen across every building and phase uses the exact same tile — no mismatched lots between phase one and phase three. Delivery is staged buildings- or floors-at-a-time to match your construction schedule, so material shows up as crews are ready for it rather than clogging the site. One insured partner, one point of contact, from the first estimate through the last unit.

Apartment & multifamily tile FAQ

Do you offer volume pricing for multi-unit projects?
Yes. Apartment and multifamily projects are priced by volume at builder/wholesale rates, so cost-per-unit stays low across many identical bathrooms and kitchens. Send the unit count and scope and we'll quote.
Is the tile durable enough for rental turnover?
We spec materials that hold up to tenant turnover and heavy common-area use — porcelain and large-format surfaces that resist wear and clean up easily, so units stay rent-ready with minimal maintenance.
Can you keep the look consistent across buildings and phases?
Yes. We hold inventory so the same tile runs across every unit and phase, and we stage delivery to match your construction schedule — no mismatched dye lots or scrambling for more mid-project.
