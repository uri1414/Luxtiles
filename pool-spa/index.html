Choosing pool & spa tile

Glass waterlineNon-porous, UV-stable, and luminous in the water — the classic waterline choice and our specialty in custom blends.
PorcelainA durable, budget-friendly waterline option when it's rated for submersion and freeze/chemical exposure.
Steps & shelvesTextured finishes add grip where feet land — worth specifying on steps, benches, and sun shelves.
Feature & spillwayBold mosaics and stone accents for raised walls, spillways, and water features that become the focal point.

One tip that saves money: confirm the tile is rated for continuous submersion and your climate before ordering. An interior-only tile can fail at a waterline. We flag that up front so you don't pay for it later.

Caring for pool & spa tile

Waterline tile lives right at the mineral line, so a little routine care keeps it looking new. Wipe the waterline during regular pool maintenance before scale and oils harden into a band; keep water chemistry balanced, since unbalanced water is what etches and scales tile fastest; and lift stubborn calcium with a soft brush or a pumice stone made for pools rather than anything that can scratch glass. Because the glass and rated porcelain we supply are non-porous, they never need sealing — the upkeep is really about the water, not the tile. Re-tiling an older pool? We can help you choose a waterline that hides scale better and coordinates with your plaster or pebble finish.

Pool & spa tile FAQ

What tile is best for a pool waterline?
Glass tile and vitreous (frost- and chemical-resistant) porcelain are the standard for waterlines because they don't absorb water, resist pool chemicals and sunlight, and clean up easily where scale tends to form. Glass in particular holds its color and gives that jewel-like look in the water. We help you choose a tile rated for constant submersion rather than an interior-only product.
Can you match tile to an existing pool or spa?
Often, yes. Bring photos and, if you can, a loose piece or the size and color of the existing tile. Exact discontinued matches aren't always possible, but we can usually find a close blend or a complementary band that reads intentional rather than mismatched.
Do you supply spa and step tile too?
Yes — waterline, spillways, raised spa walls, steps, and sun-shelf accents. For steps and shelves we can point you to textured options that stay grippier underfoot when wet.
Do you offer wholesale pricing and job-site delivery for pools?
Yes. Pool and spa tile is priced at wholesale/builder rates and delivered free to your job site across Greater Los Angeles, so it's on site when your pool contractor needs it.
