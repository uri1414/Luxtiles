A feature wall is where a room stops being finished and starts being memorable — the fireplace that anchors a living room, the stone behind a reception desk, the textured wall a hotel lobby is remembered for. Indoor and outdoor feature walls

Not every feature wall lives in a living room. Outdoors — around a fire feature, an entry, a pool cabana, or a commercial facade — the material has to take sun and temperature swings, so we steer those projects toward frost- and UV-stable porcelain and natural stone rated for exterior use rather than an interior-only tile. Indoors you have more freedom, and lighting becomes the design tool: grazing light across a textured or stacked-stone wall exaggerates its depth and shadow, while even, soft light flatters large-format panels and polished stone. Tell us where the wall lives and how it's lit, and we'll keep you to materials that still look right in that setting years from now.

Feature wall FAQ

What tile works best for a feature wall?
Almost anything can work on a dry interior feature wall, which is what makes them fun: large-format stone-look panels for a seamless statement, textured or 3D tile for shadow and depth, glass mosaics for shimmer, or stacked/ledger stone for a natural, tactile look. The main question is scale and lighting — we help you pick something that reads well from across the room and in your wall's light.
Can you supply tile for a fireplace surround?
Yes. Fireplace surrounds are a popular feature-wall project. For the immediate firebox area, we'll point you to heat-appropriate, non-combustible materials and keep decorative-only options where they belong. Share the fireplace type and clearances and we'll match accordingly.
What's the difference between stacked stone and large-format panels?
Stacked (ledger) stone gives a rugged, three-dimensional, natural texture assembled from small strips — great for warmth and craft. Large-format panels give a smooth, modern, near-seamless surface with minimal grout lines, ideal for a clean architectural statement. Different looks; we stock both.
Do you deliver feature-wall tile and offer wholesale pricing?
Yes — wholesale/builder pricing and job-site delivery across Greater Los Angeles, whether it's a single accent wall or feature walls across a commercial lobby.
